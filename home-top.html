The DreamEngine position on AI

No AI writes your story.

AICK for Authors is for authors who write their own books.

We do not condone the use of AI in the creative process of storytelling. We are against prompts such as “here is my idea, write it in the style of a named author”. Nothing replaces the storytelling ideas of a human being.

AI has changed book publishing for good, and there is no going back. Books are now being written by AI, and every author is competing with them. That makes it essential that authors declare the story is theirs, not AI’s.

We cannot stop AI being used to write books. What we can do is use what AI is good at to improve stories that humans have written. It works the way a developmental editor works: it points out what could be better, and the author decides what to change and writes it.

We feel for the editors, book designers and cover designers whose work has been hit hardest. We believe the way forward is to bring AI into their workflows, not to turn away from it, and AICK for Authors helps authors and professionals do exactly that. The human element is what makes a book great, and it stays with those who adapt.
