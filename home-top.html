The DreamEngine position on AI

No AI writes your story.

AICK for Authors is for authors who write their own books.

What we are against

AI writing your story

We do not condone the use of AI in the creative process of storytelling. We are against prompts such as:

“Here is my idea. Write it in the style of a named author.”

Nothing replaces the storytelling ideas of a human being.

What we do instead

AI that works like a developmental editor

We cannot stop AI being used to write books. What we can do is use what AI is good at to improve stories that humans have written.

It points out what could be better. The author decides what to change, and writes it.

For editors, book designers and cover designers

We feel for the professionals whose work has been hit hardest. We believe the way forward is to bring AI into your workflow, not to turn away from it, and AICK for Authors helps authors and professionals do exactly that. The human element is what makes a book great, and it stays with those who adapt.
