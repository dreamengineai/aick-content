Questions authors ask

What do I need to use the kit?

Claude App for Windows or Mac and Claude Pro or above. The Post Writing Engine also needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. It does not work on a phone.

Does the AI write my book?

No. Edit your book lists every error and where it is, and never rewrites. You make every change yourself. Posts, bios and launch material are drafted in your voice from your kit, and you approve what is used.

What does the Post Writing Engine cost?

It is included with AICK for Authors at no extra cost for a limited time only. You use your own Anthropic API key, which Anthropic bills to you directly. A post typically costs a fraction of a penny.

Is my work safe?

Your files stay on your computer. Claude works on copies of what you share with it, and whether Anthropic may use chats to improve its models is a setting you control in your Claude account. The Post Writing Engine sends Claude only the parts of your kit a post needs, and never your finances or contacts.

What happens after I pay?

You come straight back to the page, add your name and download your own kit with the 1-click app. You then get our downloads page, where you download all 18 skills.

Yes. You have 14 days, no questions asked. Use our contact page, choose request a refund and give your reason.
